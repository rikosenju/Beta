20x20 ORC HOUSE MAP
====================
Open index.html in a browser.

CONTROLS
- WASD / Arrow keys: move
- E: enter/exit the house when near the doorway
- Mobile: on-screen arrows + E

The player uses the supplied Orc3 idle/walk sprite sheets from the earlier Orc mob demo.
The house, grass details, interior palette, and door asset come from the supplied Craftpix asset pack.

The overworld is exactly 20x20 logical tiles (16px each).
The house has a separate walkable interior scene, so the doorway is actually usable.
